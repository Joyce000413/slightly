Concept

Slightly is not a tea brand.
It is a daily ritual.

A two-minute check-in with yourself. Your mood finds its colour, its flavour, its quiet moment.

Diagnose your mood

Gentle questions about your season, your energy, your heart. Not “which tea?” — “how are you right now?”

Your colour, your blend

Mood maps to colour. Colour maps to a handcrafted tea-fruit combination. The system chooses; you simply enjoy.

Archive the day
